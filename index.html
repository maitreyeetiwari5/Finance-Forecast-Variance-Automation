Threshold calibration

Thresholds are set per line item, not flat across the board - a line item's own historical volatility sets the bar for what counts as unusual. They were calibrated using a robust statistic (median + 1.2× scaled MAD) rather than mean/standard deviation, since std is distorted by the very outlier months this is meant to catch.

The calibration was validated against 6 known scripted anomalies in the underlying data: the chosen threshold set catches all 6, while cutting the overall flag rate from an initial 34.8% (hardcoded materiality thresholds) down to the rate shown above.
